The distinction
Today, #2 works like this:
YOU make the decision
      ↓
"Add spam@foo.com to permanent delete"
      ↓
Codex interprets your instruction
      ↓
Gmail AI Manager executes it

Agentic #3 becomes:
AI examines inbox
      ↓
AI reasons about messages/senders
      ↓
AI proposes actions
      ↓
YOU approve higher-impact actions
      ↓
existing deterministic tools execute

Eventually, low-risk decisions could be automated:
Observe → Reason → Decide → Act → Verify
                    ↓
             Gmail AI Manager
